ASCII Aquarium

Draw a fish, drop it in, feed it. An ASCII tank where drawn fish swim, eat, grow, quarrel, fall in love and lay eggs.

Click the water to drop food. Click a fish to poke it, drag it to move it. Hold the cursor still and fish come to look. Golden scales buy decor and lights. Keys: D draw, F feed, M sound, Escape close.
