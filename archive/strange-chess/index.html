Strange Chess

Chess with broken physics, played as a run against four bosses on a night meadow made of grass strokes and ASCII: a teleporting knight, a bouncing bishop, a bulldozer rook, wind, and ground that crumbles into holes.

A 6×6 board. The Knight teleports through the centre of the board, the Bishop bounces once off an edge, the Rook is a bulldozer that pushes whatever it moves into. Every third round the wind drifts every piece one square, and every square crumbles each time a piece leaves it. Tip the scale by five to win a fight. Select a piece with the pointer or a tap, then a square. R restarts the fight, Escape opens the menu, P toggles push mode for pieces with the pusher sigil, M toggles sound.
