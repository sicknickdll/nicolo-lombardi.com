Strange Chess

Chess with broken physics in a night meadow drawn in ASCII: knights mirror through the board, rooks bulldoze, bishops bounce, the wind pushes, the ground erodes into holes. A run of four bosses, two candles, one scale to tip.

A 6 by 6 board. The Knight (Glitch) teleports to the square mirrored through the centre of the board, or steps one square. The Bishop (Light ray) bounces once off an edge. The Rook (Bulldozer) never captures: it pushes every piece in its way one square on, and pieces pushed off the board or into a hole are destroyed. Every three rounds the wind blows and every piece drifts a square. Squares wear away as pieces leave them, until they become holes. Destroying pieces tips a scale: five in your favour wins the fight. Four bosses: the Trapper, the Angler, the Prospector and the Woodcarver, whose Moon must be pushed into a hole. Play with the mouse, touch or the keyboard: arrow keys to move over the board, Enter or Space to select and move, R to restart a fight, Escape to pause, M for sound.
