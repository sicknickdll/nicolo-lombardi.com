The Tarot Reader

An interactive tarot reading by candlelight: a fortune teller in the dark, twenty-one Major Arcana illustrated for this archive, a guiding card and a cross of five.

Shuffle the deck, cut it, meet the guiding card at the bottom of the deck, then choose five cards for your past, present, future, shadow and conscious potential. Use the mouse, touch, or the keyboard: Enter or Space to continue, arrow keys to choose, Escape to put a card back, M for sound.
