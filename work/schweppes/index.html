About the project

A fully AI-generated project for Schweppes. Guided by Clive, the film invites viewers into the iconic Schweppes mansion to celebrate New Year’s Eve in a world where elegance, fantasy, and generative AI come together to create a unique festive experience. I contributed to the storyboarding and frame generation, and made the final VO and voice design of Clive. Property of WPP Production / WPP Holding. Featured here to showcase my contribution as part of the production and creative team.
